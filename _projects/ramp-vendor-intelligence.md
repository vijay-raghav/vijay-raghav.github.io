# Ramp Vendor Intelligence

[Explore the interactive concept →]({{ '/ramp/' | relative_url }})

A product strategy and interactive prototype exploring how Ramp can connect contract evidence, comparable pricing, and vendor alternatives when a customer is about to renew or commit spend.

Three decision moments: resolve conflicting terms, compare the deal and alternatives, then carry the evidence into approval. Includes the product bet, success metrics, marketplace incentives, and reliability boundaries.
